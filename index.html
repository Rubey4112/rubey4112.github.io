About

Junior electrical engineer at George Mason.

I am a junior in the B.S. electrical engineering program at George Mason University. I expect to graduate in December 2028, and my GPA is 3.97. Most of my time goes to robotics, embedded systems, PCB design, and FPGA design in VHDL.

Through August 2026 I was an undergraduate research assistant at the Mason Autonomy and Robotics Center, working on swarm robotics platforms. That summer I was also lead instructor and course developer for GMU ACCESS Academy, a robotics camp for high school students, and a Tech Fellow for CodePath’s Foundations of AI Engineering.

This fall I am treasurer of the IEEE Student Branch at George Mason University and an electrical engineer in NASA L'SPACE Mission Concept Academy, a 15-week virtual program that runs through preliminary design review.
